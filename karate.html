Frequently Asked Questions

Karate Software & Tournament Organization FAQ

Everything you need to know about setting up digital scoring, coach verification, student belt records, and complete tournament management.

Q How does Crux Studios enable 100% paperless karate tournaments from registrations to prizes?

Our software suite delivers a 100% paperless, zero-paper tournament experience from start to finish. EntryDesk handles student registrations, entry fees, and coach verifications without physical forms. RingFlow powers live tatami scoring on touchpads, automated draw brackets, and instant podium results for medals, certificates, and prize distribution with zero paper delays.

Q How does live tatami and referee match scoring work in RingFlow?

RingFlow operates in real-time across tablets, laptops, and ringside monitors. Table officials and referees record points, warnings, fouls, and Senshu instantly on touchpads, updating the public match scoreboard and tournament bracket simultaneously with zero match delays.

Q How do karate coaches verify student tournament entries in EntryDesk?

Students or parents submit tournament entry applications independently through their personal student profile. Each application routes directly to their designated sensei or coach dashboard for verification. Once the coach verifies eligibility, the verified roster forwards directly to tournament organizers.

Q What software tracks martial arts student belt promotions and grading history?

HonorLog is a dedicated student database and digital belt registry for martial arts academies. It archives complete student profiles from White to Black belt, promotion exam dates, sensei grading feedback, and tournament medal cabinets.

Q Can Crux Studios deploy this karate software for our dojo or upcoming tournament?

Yes. Crux Studios provides complete setup, customized deployments, and dedicated support for academies, dojos, state federations, and tournament organizers. Contact us directly at contact@cruxstudios.dev for quick onboarding.
